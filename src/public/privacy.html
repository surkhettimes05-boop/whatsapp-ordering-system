Privacy Policy

Updated: 8 October 2026

This notice describes information handled by Pasalho's WhatsApp ordering service and connected shopping and administration tools. The current service is a test pilot.

Information we collect

When you use the service, we may receive your WhatsApp phone number and profile name, messages and attachments you send, product selections, cart and order details, delivery addresses or locations you provide, and support requests. The service also records technical information such as request times, IP addresses, message identifiers, delivery status and error logs. Staff account information is used to manage access.

How we use information

We use this information to respond to messages, show products, manage carts and orders, check delivery availability, arrange fulfillment, provide support, maintain order and payment records, prevent duplicate transactions and protect the service. The current pilot uses cash on delivery; it does not collect payment-card details.

Service providers and sharing

Meta/WhatsApp processes messages through the WhatsApp Business Platform. Render hosts this service and its database and cache. Information may be processed outside Nepal. Authorized staff and fulfillment personnel receive information needed to handle orders and support. We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information when required by applicable law.

Retention and security

We keep information needed for service operation, order records, support and security. You may request deletion as described below. Records needed for accounting, unresolved orders, disputes or applicable legal obligations may be retained. We use access controls and technical safeguards, but no online service can guarantee complete security.

Your choices

You can stop messaging the service and request access, correction or deletion of your information by emailing surkhettimes05@gmail.com. See our Data Deletion Instructions. Avoid sending passwords, payment-card information or other unnecessary sensitive information in chats.

Contact and updates

Contact Pasalho in Birendranagar, Surkhet, Nepal at surkhettimes05@gmail.com. Changes to this notice will appear on this page with an updated date.
