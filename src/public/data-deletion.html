Data Deletion Instructions

Updated: 8 October 2026

You can request deletion of personal information held by Pasalho's WhatsApp ordering service and connected shopping tools.

Send a request

  1. Email surkhettimes05@gmail.com with the subject Pasalho data deletion request.
  2. Include the WhatsApp phone number you used, with country code, and whether you want all eligible personal information deleted or a specific record removed.
  3. We may ask you to confirm control of that phone number before acting on the request. Do not send passwords, access tokens or identity documents unless specifically needed and requested through an appropriate channel.

What happens next

We review the request and delete or anonymize eligible account, conversation, address and shopping information. We will explain any records retained to resolve an active order or dispute, maintain accounting records or meet applicable legal obligations, and confirm the outcome by email. Removing information from Pasalho does not delete copies in your own WhatsApp chat or records independently held by Meta/WhatsApp.

Questions

For updates about your request, reply to the same email thread. Contact: Pasalho, Birendranagar, Surkhet, Nepal.
